Briefing Generator

Deutsch

Choose one of the 12 templates, fill in what you already know and save the result as a fillable PDF. Empty fields remain input fields in the PDF, so the template turns into a questionnaire your agency can complete on screen.

All input stays in your browser. Nothing is stored or transmitted. It is lost when you reload the page.
